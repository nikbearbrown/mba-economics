# Principles of Economics
## Compact Tik TOC Architecture

**Book type:** MBA / business economics textbook  
**Status:** Retrospective architecture for developed content  

## Core Thesis

This book teaches economics for managers: how markets, incentives, costs, competition, macro conditions, and policy shape business decisions.

## Primary Reader

MBA students, managers, founders, and professionals who need economics as a decision tool.

## Learning Arc

1. **Managerial microeconomics:** demand, supply, elasticity, costs, pricing, competition, and market power.
2. **Strategic and policy contexts:** externalities, regulation, labor, inequality, trade, and platforms.
3. **Macroeconomic environment:** growth, inflation, unemployment, interest rates, fiscal/monetary policy, and global shocks.

## Chapter Architecture

Each chapter should begin with a business decision affected by an economic force, then introduce the model and its limits.

## Terminal Capability

The reader can use economic reasoning to interpret markets, forecast decision consequences, and challenge weak business claims.

## Adoption Risks

- Scope may be too broad for MBA pacing.
- Ideological balance matters.
- AI-generated policy claims require evidence checks.
