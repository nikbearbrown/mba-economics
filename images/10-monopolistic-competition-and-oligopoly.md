## Figure 1 — Monopolistic competitor's long-run equilibrium
**Concept.** Cost curves for a single firm: U-shaped LRAC, downward-sloping demand curve tangent to the LRAC at a point *to the left* of LRAC's minimum. MR = MC at this output. Annotation labels the excess capacity gap and the zero-profit condition.
**Where:** in §"Monopolistic competition — many firms, differentiated products."

## Figure 2 — Perfect competition vs. monopolistic competition long-run equilibrium
**Concept.** Two side-by-side panels. Both show LRAC and a horizontal-or-near-horizontal demand line. Left: perfectly competitive — demand line tangent to bottom of U. Right: monopolistic competition — demand line tangent to falling portion. Annotation: "Mon comp trades efficiency for variety."
**Where:** in §"Monopolistic competition," at the end.

**Where:** in §"The prisoner's dilemma."
