# Image briefs — Chapter 28: Monetary Policy and Bank Regulation

## Hero image
**Concept.** A close-up of a Federal Reserve building entrance, with the eagle seal visible. Mood: institutional gravity. No people.

## Figure 1 — Fed structure organizational chart
**Concept.** Three boxes: Board of Governors (7 members), 12 Regional Reserve Banks, FOMC (12 voting members). Lines showing how they connect.
**Where:** in §"How the Fed is structured."

## Figure 2 — Federal funds rate history 1980–2024
**Concept.** Time series. Y-axis: federal funds rate %. X-axis: 1980–2024. Visible: high rates 1980–82 (Volcker), declining trend through 1990s and 2000s, near-zero 2008–2015, rising mid-2010s, near-zero again 2020, sharp rise 2022–2023.
**Where:** in §"Expansionary vs. contractionary policy" or §"The zero lower bound problem."

## Figure 3 — Fed balance sheet 2007–2024
**Concept.** Time series. Y-axis: total assets ($ trillions). X-axis: 2007–2024. Visible: ~$0.9T pre-2008, jump to $4.5T by 2014, plateau, sharp rise 2020–22 to ~$9T, gradual decline 2022–24. Annotations marking QE1, QE2, QE3, QE4 (COVID).
**Where:** in §"The zero lower bound problem."

## Figure 4 — Equation of exchange decomposition
**Concept.** A simple equation visualization: M × V = P × Q, with each term shown as a stylized bar. Two scenarios side by side: 2008–14 (M up, V down, P stable, Q up modestly) and 2020–22 (M up, V up, P up, Q up). The contrast shows how velocity and supply conditions interact with money supply.
**Where:** in §"Velocity of money."
