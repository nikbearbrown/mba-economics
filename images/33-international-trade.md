# Image briefs — Chapter 33: International Trade

## Hero image
**Concept.** A close-up of an iPhone box, opened, with the familiar "Designed by Apple in California — Assembled in China" text visible. Mood: a single product whose value comes from many places. No people.

## Figure 1 — U.S./Brazil PPFs
**Concept.** Two side-by-side PPFs. U.S.: wheat axis large, sugar axis modest. Brazil: sugar axis large, wheat axis small. Slope of U.S. PPF (opportunity cost) is shallow (low cost of wheat in sugar terms); Brazil's is steep. Annotation showing the comparative-advantage difference.
**Where:** in §"The gains from trade — a worked example."

## Figure 2 — Production with vs. without trade
**Concept.** Bar chart showing total world output of wheat and sugar. Without trade: 400 wheat + 350 sugar. With trade specialization: 600 wheat + 300 sugar. Net world gain visible.
**Where:** in §"The gains from trade."

## Figure 3 — Intra-industry trade flow chart
**Concept.** Schematic showing U.S. and Germany. Arrows in both directions: U.S. cars to Germany, German cars to U.S. Annotation: "Same product category; different varieties."
**Where:** in §"Modern trade — intra-industry and supply chains."

## Figure 4 — iPhone global supply chain map
**Concept.** Stylized world map with arrows pointing to a central iPhone icon: chips from Taiwan, displays from Korea, batteries from Japan, assembly in China, design from California, components from many other countries.
**Where:** in cold open or §"Modern trade."
