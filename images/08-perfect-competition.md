# Image briefs — Chapter 8: Perfect Competition

## Hero image
**Concept.** A wheat field at sunrise, viewed from low angle so individual stalks are in focus and the field stretches to the horizon. A grain silo in the distance. Mood: a market made of identical individual sellers. No people.

## Figure 1 — The price taker's demand curve
**Concept.** Two side-by-side panels. Left: market supply-demand diagram with equilibrium price P*. Right: a single firm with horizontal demand at P*. Annotation: "the firm can sell as much as it wants at P*; it cannot move the market."
**Where:** in §"How a price taker decides."

## Figure 2 — Profit-maximization at P = MC
**Concept.** Cost curves (MC, ATC, AVC) for a single firm. Horizontal line at price P*. Mark the optimal quantity Q* where P = MC. Shade the profit rectangle: width Q*, height (P − ATC at Q*).
**Where:** in §"How a price taker decides."

## Figure 3 — Three short-run cases: profit, loss, shutdown
**Concept.** Three small panels, same firm cost curves, three different prices.
- Panel A: high price, P > ATC, profit rectangle shaded.
- Panel B: medium price, AVC < P < ATC, loss rectangle shaded but firm still produces.
- Panel C: low price, P < AVC, firm shuts down (Q = 0).
**Where:** in §"The shutdown rule."

## Figure 4 — Long-run zero-profit equilibrium
**Concept.** Cost curves for a single firm with horizontal demand at P* tangent to the minimum of LRAC. P = MC = min LRAC. Annotation: "Long-run equilibrium under perfect competition. Each firm produces at minimum average cost. Economic profit = 0."
**Where:** in §"The long run — entry and exit."
