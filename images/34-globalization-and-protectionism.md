# Image briefs — Chapter 34: Globalization and Protectionism

## Hero image
**Concept.** A cargo container with international shipping markings, half in shadow. A small flag of nondescript origin painted on the side. Mood: trade flows that could be moved or stopped by political decisions. No people.

## Figure 1 — Tariff supply-demand diagram
**Concept.** Standard supply-demand chart for a domestically-produced good with imports. Domestic supply, world supply (horizontal at world price), domestic demand. With tariff: world supply shifts up by tariff amount. Show: new equilibrium domestic price, new domestic production, reduced imports, tariff revenue rectangle, deadweight loss triangles.
**Where:** in §"How protectionism works."

## Figure 2 — Concentrated losses, dispersed gains
**Concept.** Mirror of Ch 18 figure. Left side: small number of large coins (workers in trade-affected industries). Right side: thousands of tiny coins (consumers benefiting from cheaper imports). Annotation: "Political pressure flows from the side that's organized."
**Where:** in §"How protectionism works."

## Figure 3 — China shock visualization
**Concept.** Time series showing U.S. manufacturing employment 1990–2024. Sharp acceleration of decline post-2001 (China WTO accession). Annotation showing the timing of the shock.
**Where:** in §"Trade adjustment."

## Figure 4 — U.S. trade-skeptical politics timeline
**Concept.** Timeline 1990–2024. Markers for: NAFTA 1994, China WTO 2001, China-shock empirical work 2013, Brexit June 2016, Trump tariffs 2018, Biden tariffs continuation 2021, ongoing today. Visual showing the emergence and persistence of trade-skeptical politics.
**Where:** in §"Trade institutions" or §"Synthesis."
