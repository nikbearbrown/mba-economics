# Image briefs — Chapter 1: Welcome to Economics

## Hero image
**Concept.** Close on a pair of hands at a workbench, mid-task — sorting straight pins on dark wood. A leather-bound notebook sits open beside the pins, hash marks and a circled number visible. Single warm light source from upper left. 1776 mood without literal costuming.
**Pedagogical job.** Ground the chapter in *one specific instant of human curiosity*. The pin, the notebook, the hand.
**Constraints.** No faces. No anachronistic objects. Period-appropriate writing implement (dip pen).

## Figure 1 — Pin productivity comparison
**Concept.** Pictogram-style infographic. Left: one figure, "20 pins/day." Right: ten small figures, each at a different operation, "48,000 pins/day." Below: "*Per worker:* 20 vs. 4,800 — a factor of 240."
**Where:** in §"The thing that follows from scarcity," after the "what changed?" pivot.

## Figure 2 — The circular flow diagram
**Concept.** Two large boxes — HOUSEHOLDS (left), FIRMS (right) — with the PRODUCT MARKET arching above and the FACTOR MARKET arching below. Four arrow flows: goods/services + payments (outer); labor/capital + wages/rents (inner). Two-color scheme: one for real flows, one for money flows.
**Where:** in §"The simplest model."
**Pedagogical job.** Give a memorable, drawable picture. Same labels as the prose so eye and ear match.

## Figure 3 — Three coordination systems on a spectrum
**Concept.** Horizontal axis. *Pure command* on the far left, *Pure market* on the far right. Country dots placed: North Korea (far left), Cuba, China, Russia, France, Sweden, U.S., Singapore, Hong Kong (far right). Annotation: "Neither end exists in pure form."
**Where:** in §"Three ways to coordinate."

## Style sheet (chapter-wide)
- Two colors plus a neutral. Same accent across all figures in this chapter.
- Body labels in a humanist sans (Inter, Source Sans). Numbers in tabular monospace.
- Captions open with what the reader is looking at, then what to take away.
- Readable in monochrome. Color reinforces, never carries meaning alone.
- Source citation under every figure that uses external data.
