## Concept specifications
- **Bond price ↔ interest rate inverse.** When rates rise, existing bond prices fall.
- **Stock = residual claim**, not a fixed cash flow.
- **Risk-return trade-off.** Higher expected return = higher variance, on average.
- **Diversification reduces risk without reducing expected return.** "Only free lunch."
- **Efficient-market hypothesis** — current prices reflect public information.
- **Random walk** — past prices contain no signal about future prices.

## Named tools
- Four channels for financing: profits, bank loans, bonds, stocks.
- Bond yield calculation.
- Equity risk premium.
- Mutual funds and index funds for diversification.
- Efficient-market hypothesis (EMH).
- Random walk theory.

## Trade-offs named
- Debt vs equity (predictable cost vs upside-share).
- Diversification (reduces idiosyncratic risk; can't eliminate market risk).
- Active vs passive investing (after-fee underperformance is common).
- Regulatory protection vs financial innovation.

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "I can pick winners." → Empirical record: very rarely on average after costs.
- "Bonds are safe." → Default-risk-low Treasuries still have interest-rate risk.
- "Diversification is just for cowards." → No, it's the only free lunch in finance.
