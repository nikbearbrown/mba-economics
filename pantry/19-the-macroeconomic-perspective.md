## Concept specifications
- **GDP = market value of final goods & services produced within a country.**
- **Final ≠ all transactions.** Intermediates excluded.
- **Three measurement approaches** (expenditure, income, production) all give same total.
- **Real GDP ≠ nominal GDP.** Real strips out inflation.
- **GDP per capita ≠ welfare.** Misses non-market production, distribution, externalities, leisure, sustainability.
- **PPP ≠ market exchange rate.** PPP corrects for cross-country price-level differences.

## Trade-offs named
- GDP simplicity and comparability vs. completeness as a welfare measure.
- Market production captured vs. household production missed.
- Static measure of present production vs. forward-looking sustainability concerns.

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "GDP measures welfare." → Partially. Misses many things.
- "Recessions are abnormalities." → No, they're recurrent features of market economies.
- "Higher GDP growth is always better." → No, depends on what's being grown and at what cost.
