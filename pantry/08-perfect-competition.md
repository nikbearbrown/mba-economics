## Concept specifications
- **Perfect competition has 4 conditions** — many firms with identical products, many buyers and sellers, full information, free entry/exit.
- **Price taker** — firm faces horizontal demand at the market price.
- **MR = P** for a price taker; profit max therefore at P = MC.
- **Shutdown rule:** P ≥ AVC → keep producing in short run; P < AVC → shut down.
- **Long-run economic profit = 0** under perfect competition; entry/exit drives this.
- **Zero economic profit ≠ zero accounting profit.**

## Named tools
- Four conditions for perfect competition.
- P = MC profit-maximizing rule (for a price taker).
- Shutdown rule (P vs. AVC).
- Entry and exit dynamics.
- Long-run equilibrium: P = min LRAC.
- Productive and allocative efficiency in long-run equilibrium.

## Trade-offs named
- Short-run loss vs. long-run exit decision.
- Sustainability of profits (zero-profit attractor under free entry) vs. ability to invest in R&D, etc.
- Idealized model power vs. real-world departures.

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Zero economic profit means firms are dying." → No. It means competitive return.
- "Perfect competition is everywhere." → No, it's rare. It's a benchmark.
- "Shutdown means closing forever." → No. Short-run shutdown vs. long-run exit are different decisions.
