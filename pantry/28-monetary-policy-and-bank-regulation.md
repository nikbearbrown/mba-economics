# Pantry — Chapter 28: Monetary Policy and Bank Regulation

- Expansionary policy chain (rate cut → invest/consume → AD shift).
- Equation of exchange and inflation calculation.

## Concept specifications
- **Fed = Board + 12 regional banks + FOMC.**
- **Dual mandate:** maximum employment + stable prices.
- **OMO is the main tool**; discount rate and reserve requirements are supplementary.
- **Zero lower bound** = traditional monetary policy hits a floor at ~0% interest rates.
- **QE = unconventional tool** for the ZLB era.
- **Quantity theory** holds in long run; velocity fluctuates short run.

## Trade-offs named
- Independence (technical decisions) vs. democratic accountability.
- Tight money to fight inflation vs. recession costs.
- Bank capital requirements (safety) vs. lending capacity (growth).
- QE (monetary tool) vs. fiscal authority (limit on Fed).

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Fed is too powerful." → Powerful but constrained by mandate, statute, institutional norms.
- "Money creation = inflation." → Long run yes; short run can be offset by velocity changes.
- "QE is just printing money." → No, asset purchases swap reserves for bonds; transmission is through long-term rates and asset prices.
