## Concept specifications
- **Appreciation = currency rises.** Depreciation = currency falls.
- **Demand and supply for currency** determine the rate.
- **PPP** anchors long-run rates; not short-run.
- **Trilemma**: pick 2 of {capital mobility, fixed rate, monetary independence}.
- **Weaker currency is expansionary** (boost exports, raise import prices).

## Named tools
- Foreign exchange (FX) market.
- Trade-weighted exchange rate.
- Purchasing power parity (PPP).
- Big Mac Index.
- Floating, soft peg, hard peg, currency union.
- Trilemma in international finance.

## Trade-offs named
- Floating (volatility) vs. peg (stability + lost monetary policy).
- Currency union (max integration) vs. tailored monetary policy.
- Defending peg (uses reserves) vs. abandoning peg (loss of credibility).

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Strong currency = strong economy." → Sometimes; depends on context.
- "Pegs always work." → Many have collapsed; depend on fundamentals and credibility.
- "PPP determines exchange rates." → Long run yes, short run no.
