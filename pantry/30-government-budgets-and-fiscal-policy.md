## Concept specifications
- **Mandatory ≠ discretionary** spending.
- **Annual deficit ≠ accumulated debt.** Deficit adds to debt each year.
- **Three tax types** based on income progressivity: regressive, proportional, progressive.
- **Three lags** in fiscal policy: recognition, decision, implementation.
- **Balanced-budget amendment** would prevent counter-cyclical fiscal action.

## Named tools
- Federal/state/local budget structure.
- Mandatory vs. discretionary spending.
- Tax classification (progressive, proportional, regressive).
- Annual deficit, accumulated debt.
- Expansionary/contractionary fiscal policy.
- Automatic stabilizers (revisited from Ch 25).
- Three lags of fiscal policy.

## Trade-offs named
- Discretionary policy responsiveness vs. lag costs.
- Balanced-budget rule discipline vs. flexibility for stabilization.
- Tax progressivity (equity) vs. efficiency (work disincentives).
- Mandatory programs (long-run commitment) vs. flexibility.

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Federal budget like household budget." → No, very different constraints.
- "All taxes are regressive." → No, federal income tax is progressive.
- "Balanced budget every year." → No, would prevent counter-cyclical policy.
