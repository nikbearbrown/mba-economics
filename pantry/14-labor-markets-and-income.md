## Concept specifications
- **Marginal revenue product (MRP) = marginal product × output price.**
- **Monopsony = one buyer of labor.** Marginal cost of labor > wage; firm hires fewer workers and pays less than competitive level.
- **Minimum wage in monopsonistic market** can *increase* both wages and employment.
- **Union acts like labor monopolist** — restricts supply, raises wages, fewer hired.
- **Bilateral monopoly** outcome depends on bargaining power.

## Trade-offs named
- Union: insider gains vs outsider exclusion.
- Immigration: aggregate gains vs concentrated wage effects on competing groups.
- Discrimination: market self-correction limited by market power and information.
- Minimum wage: traditional employment risk vs monopsony-correcting effect.

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Markets eliminate discrimination" → Partial only; market power, customer/coworker preferences, and information barriers preserve it.
- "Unions help all workers" → Insiders yes; outsiders less clearly; depends on context.
- "Immigration always lowers native wages" → Aggregate effect is small; concentrated effects on directly competing groups can be larger.
