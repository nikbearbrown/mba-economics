## Concept specifications
- **Sticky prices/wages** = adjustment slow enough that demand shocks cause real output effects.
- **Expenditure multiplier** = 1/(1-MPC) in simplest form; smaller with leakages.
- **Automatic stabilizers** = built-in counter-cyclical features of tax/transfer system.
- **Phillips curve** short-run (trade-off) vs long-run (vertical at natural rate).

## Trade-offs named
- Discretionary policy responsiveness vs. lag/political costs.
- Multiplier size depends on cycle position and leakages.
- Permanent inflation-unemployment trade-off (no) vs. short-run (yes).

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Markets always self-correct quickly." → No, sticky prices delay adjustment.
- "Stimulus = magic multiplier." → No, real-world multipliers are modest.
- "Phillips curve gives a permanent menu." → No, Friedman-Phelps showed it shifts.
