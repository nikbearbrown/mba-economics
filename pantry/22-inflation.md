## Concept specifications
- **Inflation = rate of change of general price level.**
- **CPI = basket-cost index.** Substitution, quality, new-goods biases overstate true inflation.
- **PCE Price Index** (Fed's preferred) lower than CPI by ~0.3% on average.
- **Indexed = automatically adjusted for inflation.**
- **Hyperinflation = >50% per month sustained.** Driven by fiscal-monetary fusion.

## Trade-offs named
- Inflation tax (helps debtors, hurts creditors).
- Indexing reduces some inflation costs but transfers others.
- Low and stable inflation target vs. flexibility for shocks.
- Fed credibility vs. political pressure to monetize debt.

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Inflation is always bad." → Mild and stable inflation has costs but they're small.
- "CPI accurately measures the cost of living." → Has known biases that probably overstate.
- "Hyperinflation is mysterious." → It's not — it's monetary financing of fiscal deficits.
