## Concept specifications
- **Implicit cost ≠ explicit cost.** Implicit = opportunity cost of resources used but not paid for in cash.
- **Accounting profit ≠ economic profit.** The latter subtracts implicit costs.
- **Short run = at least one fixed input.** Long run = all inputs variable.
- **Marginal pulls average.** MC > average → average rises; MC < average → average falls; MC crosses average at the average's minimum.

## Named tools
- Production function Q = f(L, K).

## Trade-offs named
- Scale benefits vs. coordination costs (small firms vs. large firms).
- Fixed-cost-heavy structures (winner-take-most) vs. variable-cost-heavy structures (fragmented industries).
- Specialization gains vs. flexibility (revisits Ch 1's specialization brittleness).

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Free businesses have zero costs." → No. Implicit costs are real.
- "Big firms are always more efficient." → No. Diseconomies of scale do exist.
- "Amazon undercut everyone by being willing to lose money." → Partly. The deeper reason is cost structure.
