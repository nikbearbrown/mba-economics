## Concept specifications
- **Unemployed = no job + available + looking.** Excludes "out of labor force."
- **Unemployment rate = unemployed / labor force.** Not / population.
- **Labor-force participation rate** captures dropouts the unemployment rate misses.
- **U-6** broader measure: includes discouraged workers and involuntary part-time.
- **Sticky wages** prevent labor market from clearing in recession.
- **Natural rate** = frictional + structural; varies over time.

## Trade-offs named
- Wage flexibility (less unemployment in recession) vs. wage stability (worker welfare and morale).
- Frictional unemployment (healthy mobility) vs. long-duration unemployment (welfare loss).
- Inflation pressure (below natural rate) vs. recession pressure (above natural rate).

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Unemployment rate captures the labor market." → Misses LFP changes and underemployment.
- "Wages should adjust to clear the market." → They don't; sticky wages have multiple structural causes.
- "Natural rate is a fixed number." → No, it varies with demographics, institutions, technology.
