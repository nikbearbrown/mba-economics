## Concept specifications
- **Absolute advantage** = produces more of a good per unit of input.
- **Comparative advantage** = produces a good at lower opportunity cost.
- **Trade gains depend on different opportunity costs**, not different absolute productivity.
- **Intra-industry trade** dominates modern trade flows (similar countries trading similar goods).
- **Dynamic gains** include productivity transfer, technology spillovers, scale economies.

## Trade-offs named
- Static gains (Ricardian) vs. dynamic gains (over time).
- Specialization benefits vs. concentrated displacement of workers.
- Open trade (gains) vs. national policy autonomy.

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Better-at-everything country shouldn't trade." → False. Comparative advantage holds.
- "All trade is between rich and poor countries." → No, most modern trade is intra-industry between similar economies.
- "Trade only matters for finished goods." → No, supply chain fragmentation is central.
