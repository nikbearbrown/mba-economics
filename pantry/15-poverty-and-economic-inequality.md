## Concept specifications
- **Poverty ≠ inequality.** Different questions, different measures.
- **Poverty line:** Orshansky 1960s method (food×3, inflation-adjusted).
- **Quintile shares + Lorenz curve + Gini coefficient** as standard inequality measures.
- **Gini = 0** perfect equality, **Gini = 1** one household has everything.
- **Poverty trap:** high benefit-reduction rate creates effective marginal tax of ~100%.
- **EITC reverses the trap** by subsidizing earnings.

## Trade-offs named
- Generosity of safety net vs. work incentive (poverty trap).
- Pre-tax inequality (labor markets) vs. post-tax inequality (redistribution).
- Universal vs. targeted programs (administrative cost vs. targeting precision).
- Equality vs. efficiency in tax design.

## Counter-claims taken positions on
- "Inequality and poverty are the same thing." → No, distinct.
- "Welfare destroys work incentive." → Sometimes (poverty trap); EITC structurally avoids it.
- "Redistribution always reduces growth." → Not for human-capital investments; often overstated for transfers.
