## Companion files

Each chapter has three companion files in dedicated folders:

- `pantry/[chapter-slug].md` — reusable ingredients (scenes, examples, vocabulary, named tools, trade-offs).
- `images/[chapter-slug].md` — figure briefs (hero image + in-chapter figures with style sheet).
- `bookmaps/[chapter-slug].md` — source map (which OpenStax module fed which section, what was promoted/demoted/cut, what's deferred).

## Voice anchor

The voice for the entire book is set by Bear's rewrite of Chapter 1, preserved at `style/canonical-chapter-1.md`. Subsequent chapters were drafted to match its register: tagline subtitle, scene-first cold open, named-section transitions, three concept arc per chapter, woven worked examples, five LLM Exercises at the end, "What comes next" + "What would change my mind" + "Still puzzling" closers, italicized byline.
